The Mind's Theatre

Approximate Reading Time : 3 minutes

Summary

Humans are interesting creatures. They believe in so many things. They believe they have to do so many things. They believe so much depend on them.

They fight battles in their own minds for an audience that exists only in their heads. How much ever they fight, however many victories they gain, they always find a new hurdle, a new enemy. The reason these enemies gather in their minds is because of this audience. They become a gladiator in a colosseum filled with people who judge them, some real, some not.
