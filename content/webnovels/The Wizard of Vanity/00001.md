# Rumours of the Wizard

Do you believe the words of other people ?
If so, why ?

There are many that were led astray by it, and yet they learn not.

These were the words of some such strangers.

It seems that in the mountains far to the north, there lives a wizard.
This man is said to have gone beyond all mortal limits, has mastered everything there is to know, has every strength that one could ever want.

Yet it seems he locks himself shut in the burrows of the mountains, with some rumours even stating that he lived inside a raging volcano.

Normally, the world would forget such claims, but it seems that many were fascinated with this man's existence and have searched for him in the mountains.
Yet none have found him.
This should have disproved his existence, yet for some reason, to the commonfolk, it made him more of a legend.
For whatever reason, emperors started to get anxious about this figure and sent their armies to the mountains who all returned empty handed.

The man had to be hoax. There was no other way around it.
